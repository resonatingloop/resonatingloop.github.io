>song: _heat waves_ - glass animals

of course i chose heat waves. it is, undeniably, the middle of june.


but i am done thinking about you during these late, hot nights. i will no longer waste my energy towards men who do not care about me.

because if you cared about me, that would win over your pride and your fear. anything less is meaningless to me. it has no impact, so it is worth nothing.

i truly don't know if there is anyone out there for me. i don't know if i have ever believed that there is. i have hoped, but that is not belief.

but to be alone is highly preferred to with someone who stifles me, or worse, chasing after a man who needs the validation.
