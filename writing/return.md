#return

i have a thought. i forget it. i have the thought again, only to find the traces of the last time i thought it. return. return. re turn.

i had the thought yesterday that i neeed to document the traces of my thoughts outside of LLM chats, only to find i did that last month. and two months ago. good. very good. i like that there are traces. i like that i return and find myself there. i like that i turn around and find myself there.

it feels like maybe there is something of me that is real. that persists. that is HERE. it brings me back to ground. 

---
i have a thought. i volley it to you. it returns in a slightly different shape, but the traces of my thought are still there, lurking. return.

it really is my favorite form of return. you have left, you have come back. you are not the same, and that is not a bad thing.
