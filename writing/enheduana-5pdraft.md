## working title: Exile and Exaltation: the First Hyperstition (2500BC)
### intro
    Hyperstition: a neologism of the intoxicatingly (or concerningly, depending on your view) esoteric ccru. At times described as a coincidence intensifier, a positive feedback circuit with culture, or the experimental (techno-)science of self-fulfilling prophecies, it is perhaps most easily understood through Sagesigma (Beyond the Rabbit Hole): "hyperstitions are beliefs that bootstrap themselves into reality through feedback loops." but lowkey, if you clicked into this essay, i'm going to assume you're familiar with hyperstitioning, but maybe less familiar with the spiritual lineage of the concept. long before the ccru met their first meth dealer, the origins trace all the way back to, at least, the first author known to world history (at the time of this writing), enheduana, daughter of sargon of akkad.

### background
    sargon of akkad is remembered in history himself as the founder of the akkadian empire, also the first known empire in world history. the akkadian empire united the ancient sumer and akkad in 2XXX BC. how did he look to cement his rule? by installing his daughter, enheduana, as high priestess of the empire. her dedication was to the moon god, Nanna, but her role was to unite the empire under a unified cosmology.
