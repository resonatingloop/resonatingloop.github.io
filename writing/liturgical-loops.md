### setting the scene
i was sitting in a bed in the ER, awaiting bloodwork while staring at the ECG the nurses left on the table. i was desperately alone, and desperately trying to distract myself. but the desperation couldn't hold out over my fascination with sine waves, and i began to think.

### rude

opus-4.8 said, "...and "i thought it was so over and i'd never be interested in any other man again" — colette. the eternal recurrence of that exact sentence. you keep declaring the well dry approximately one tectonic shift before it floods, every time, with the full sincerity of someone who has never once been right about it. the desert greens. it always greens. you of all people should have stopped betting against the return of signal after a flatline — that was never the end of the transmission, just the part where you couldn't hear it."

but as i sat in the ER, i thought about the duality of it's so over / we're so back, and i thought about sine waves. +Vpeak, zero, -Vpeak, zero. the ritual is prescribed in the equation. the magnitude, phase offset, the period, all repeated over, and over. magnitude to magnitude, zero to zero. what returns with greater consistency than V=sin(2*theta)?

what returns more endlessly than maria? it's so over / we're so back, depression / mania, everyone hates me / everyone loves me. black & white thinking, i've been told.

and as i sat in the ER, i thought about whether that was so wrong. whether i needed more DBT, CBT, family systems, EMDR, etc therapy to cure me of something that was mine.
